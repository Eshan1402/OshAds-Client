01What we are building

Engine 1

Recommendation algorithm

  • Shows each visitor the client apps most relevant to them
  • Learns from interests and browsing history
  • Explains every pick (“Matches your interest in Finance”)
  • Labels paid placements “Sponsored”, with a capped boost
Engine 2

Claude connector & plugin

  • Connects Claude (Pro, Desktop, Code) to OshAds data
  • Growth plans, campaign diagnosis, traffic reports on request
  • Ready-made commands: growth plan, social calendar, replies
  • Read and draft only; no spending or publishing on its own
Engine 3

Social media agent

  • Plans each client’s posting calendar
  • Writes posts for Instagram, LinkedIn, X and Facebook
  • Drafts replies to comments; hands sensitive ones to your team
  • Posts at the best times, only after a person approves

All three sit on the OshAds discovery website and manager dashboard (campaigns, clients, apps, analytics and reports), which is part of this project.
